What you sell

Acme Ramp helps B2B sales teams get new reps selling faster, with a ramp plan for each hire.

Who to sell to

B2B software companies of 50 to 300 people that are hiring their first or second sales team.

Buyers: Head of Sales, VP Sales. What 6 won and 4 lost deals have in common.

Send them

  1. Yourself, free. Copy from emails/, or load companies.csv into your own tool. For LinkedIn, send the notes in linkedin.md from your own account: this tool never touches LinkedIn.
  2. From your own mailbox with this tool. Run outlevo doctor to see what it needs, add people with outlevo contacts find or outlevo contacts import, then outlevo run. You approve every email.
  3. With Outlevo Cloud. Verified contacts and warmed-up mailboxes, ready to go, running while your laptop is closed. See Outlevo Cloud, or outlevo cloud handoff --name "My first campaign".
  4. Have Outlevo's team run it. Managed from $999 a month, or pay per booked meeting by application. See done for you.
